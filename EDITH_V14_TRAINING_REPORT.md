## 1. Honest Audit of V13 Gaps

Before initiating V14, we performed an unsparing audit of the V13 training pipeline:
1. **Memorization vs. Generalization**: V13 trained its ~72M parameter model on only ~9,760 mostly synthetic/templated samples. The reported test perplexity of `2.94` was a clear diagnostic indicator of severe memorization on uniform templates, not true conversational fluency.
2. **Missing Pretraining Foundation**: V13 went directly to conversational tuning without an underlying language foundation phase, leading to attention collapse on out-of-distribution speech.
3. **Synthetic Hallucination**: User-facing responses still relied on legacy f-string templates and database dumps because the underlying generative model was unreliable for open-domain banter.
4. **Label Contamination**: Internal intent labels (`exec_*`, `system_utility`) were mixed into the language model dataset, causing occasional internal classification strings to bleed into generated outputs.

## 4. Gate D: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) & DPO Quality Gate

Implementation: [`data_pipeline/v14/train_gate_d_sft.py`](file:///c:/Users/Aditya%20Sawant/Desktop/E.D.I.T.H/data_pipeline/v14/train_gate_d_sft.py)

### 4.1 Methodology
1. **Masked-Loss Assistant SFT**: Loss is calculated strictly on tokens between `<edith>` and `</edith>`. Prompt tokens (`<mood>...</mood><user>...</user><edith>`) are masked with `-100`.
2. **DPO Pairwise Ranking**: Pairwise preference optimization optimizing chosen persona responses against 8 categories of hard-negative candidates:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{SFT}}(\theta) - \lambda_{\text{DPO}} \log \sigma \left( \beta \cdot \left[ \log P_\theta(y_w | x) - \log P_\theta(y_l | x) \right] \right)$$
   with $\beta = 0.1$, $\lambda_{\text{DPO}} = 0.25$.

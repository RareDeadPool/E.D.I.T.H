# Project E.D.I.T.H. V14 — Data Licenses & Data Cards Directory

This document provides a comprehensive audit of all training datasets, conversational corpora, instruction sets, and authored seed data utilized in the training and evaluation of **E.D.I.T.H. V14 ("Real Voice")**.

In accordance with the E.D.I.T.H. V14 sovereign AI mandate:
- **100% Offline Runtime**: Zero cloud APIs or telemetry are invoked during inference.
- **Organic Requirement**: Strict exclusion of data generated by commercial LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) and scraped copyrighted screenplays.
- **Permissive & Transparent Licensing**: Every dataset source is audited, with non-commercial restrictions explicitly flagged.
- **Label Separation**: All dialogue training corpora are strictly scrubbed of reflex-path classifier labels (`exec_*`, `system_utility`, `network_task`, etc.) to prevent internal label leaks into dialogue.

## 2. Detailed Data Cards

### `V14-DS-01`: E.D.I.T.H. Hand-Authored Persona Seed
- **Source**: `data_pipeline/v14/authored_seed.py`
- **License**: Personal Project License
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: Establishes the core E.D.I.T.H. MCU-inspired personality: quick-witted, humbly arrogant, warm underneath, honest about missing tools, and strictly suppressing humor during user distress.
- **Content**: 2,046 hand-crafted multi-turn dialogues covering:
  - "How are you" dynamic responses across morning, afternoon, evening, and night.
  - STT acoustic mishears and ambiguity handling (*"I'll allow you"*, *"spot a fly"*, *"open crow"*).
  - Capability gaps and graceful refusals (*"Subscribe me"* $\rightarrow$ asking for channel/newsletter).
  - Dialogue repair sequences (user corrections, repetitions).
  - Self-reference memory retrieval (*"Tell me something about myself"* $\rightarrow$ natural human summary).
  - Distress disclosures and job search struggles with 100% humor suppression.

### `V14-DS-02`: Databricks Dolly-15k
- **Source**: `databricks/databricks-dolly-15k`
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC-BY-SA 3.0)
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: High-quality human-written instructions covering information extraction, brainstorming, classification, and summarization authored by Databricks employees.
- **Filtering**: Filtered for `general_qa`, `brainstorming`, and `creative_writing`. MinHash deduplicated (threshold 0.85).

### `V14-DS-03`: EmpatheticDialogues
- **Source**: Meta AI Research (`facebook/empathetic_dialogues`)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC-BY-NC 4.0)
- **Commercial Permitted**: **No (Non-Commercial Research & Personal Use)**
- **Purpose**: Teaches the model empathetic conversational turn-taking, active listening, and appropriate emotional resonance across 32 human emotional situations.
- **Filtering**: Scrubbed of PII, formatted with `<mood>warm_empathy</mood>` conditioning tokens.

### `V14-DS-04`: DailyDialog
- **Source**: `li2017dailydialog/daily_dialog` / `roskoN/dailydialog`
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0)
- **Commercial Permitted**: **No (Non-Commercial Research & Personal Use)**
- **Purpose**: Everyday casual conversation across relationships, leisure, school, tourism, and daily human problems.
- **Filtering**: Punctuation normalized, contraction apostrophes restored, MinHash deduplicated.

### `V14-DS-05`: Wikitext-103 Clean Prose
- **Source**: Salesforce Research (`Salesforce/wikitext`)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC-BY-SA 3.0)
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: High-quality Wikipedia article excerpts providing open-domain factual grounding and varied sentence structure.
- **Filtering**: Headers and citation brackets (`[1]`, `[edit]`) stripped.

### `V14-DS-06`: Project Gutenberg Public Domain Prose
- **Source**: Standard Project Gutenberg Archives
- **License**: Public Domain
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: Natural vocabulary expansion, sophisticated multi-clause English prose, and narrative structure.
- **Filtering**: Stripped Project Gutenberg headers and license footers.

### `V14-DS-07`: OpenAssistant Conversations (oasst1 human)
- **Source**: OpenAssistant Project (`OpenAssistant/oasst1`)
- **License**: Apache 2.0
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: Human-to-human conversational trees. Strictly filtered to retain only human-authored prompt and human-authored assistant branches.

### `V14-DS-08`: L3Cube HinglishChat
- **Source**: L3Cube-Pune NLP Research Group
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA 4.0)
- **Commercial Permitted**: Yes
- **Purpose**: Conversational code-mixed Hindi-English syntax (*"bhai friend sad hai kya karu"*), allowing natural bilingual dialogue.

## 3. Label Separation & Anti-Leak Verification
All datasets processed through `data_pipeline/v14/corpus_builder.py` are strictly validated with `LeakLinter`. Any sample containing classifier intent names (`exec_*`, `system_utility`, `network_task`, `respond_dialogue`, `project_manage`, `context_control`) is automatically purged. Classifier labels are exclusively reserved for the reflex-path `EdithANN` classifier and are completely absent from the generative language model corpus.
